🌿 Flora of Nepal

Endangered Plants

Nepal's mountains, forests and valleys contain valuable medicinal and rare plant species. Many of them are now threatened by excessive collection and habitat destruction.

What are endangered plants?

Endangered plants are plant species whose number has decreased greatly and which are in danger of becoming extinct (completely disappearing) in the future.
🍄

Yarsagumba

Cordyceps sinensis

A valuable Himalayan medicinal fungus that is threatened by excessive collection.

🌲

Himalayan Yew

Taxus wallichiana

A medicinal tree used for making important medicines and threatened by overharvesting.

🌸

Panchaule

Dactylorhiza hatagirea

A valuable medicinal orchid found in the Himalayan region and threatened by illegal collection.

🌾

Jatamansi

Nardostachys jatamansi

A medicinal plant whose roots are widely collected and traded.

🌱

Kutki

Neopicrorhiza scrophulariiflora

A Himalayan medicinal herb threatened by excessive harvesting.

🐾 Wildlife of Nepal

Endangered Animals

From the tropical forests of the Terai to the snowy Himalayan mountains, Nepal provides habitats for many rare and magnificent animals.

What are endangered animals?

Endangered animals are animal species whose number has decreased greatly and which are in danger of becoming extinct (completely disappearing) in the future.
🦏

One-horned Rhinoceros

Rhinoceros unicornis

A rare rhinoceros found mainly in Chitwan and Bardiya National Parks.

🐅

Bengal Tiger

Panthera tigris tigris

A powerful wild cat threatened by habitat loss and poaching.

🐼

Red Panda

Ailurus fulgens

A small and rare animal found in the Himalayan forests of Nepal.

🐆

Snow Leopard

Panthera uncia

A rare big cat living in the high Himalayan mountains of Nepal.

🐊

Gharial

Gavialis gangeticus

A rare fish-eating crocodile found in rivers such as the Narayani River.

⚠ Why Species Are Declining

Main Threats to Nepal's Biodiversity

Human activities and environmental changes are causing many plants and animals to lose their natural habitats and struggle for survival.

THREAT 01
🌳

Habitat Loss & Fragmentation

Forests, grasslands, wetlands, and other natural habitats are destroyed due to deforestation, urbanization, and development activities. When habitats are divided into smaller areas, plants and animals lose their food, shelter, and breeding places.

THREAT 02
🌡️

Climate Change

Changes in temperature and rainfall affect the natural environment of plants and animals. Some species cannot adapt quickly to these changes, which can cause their population to decline.

THREAT 03
⚠️

Overexploitation

Overexploitation means using or collecting plants and animals more than nature can replace them. Excessive hunting, fishing, logging, and collection of medicinal plants can reduce their population.

THREAT 04
🏭

Pollution

Air, water, and soil pollution can harm plants and animals and destroy their habitats. Chemicals, plastic waste, and other pollutants can make it difficult for living organisms to survive.

THREAT 05
🌿

Invasive Species

Invasive species are non-native plants or animals that spread into a new area and harm native species. They may compete with native plants and animals for food, water, and space, causing native species to decline.

🌍 OUR RESPONSIBILITY

Every Species Matters

Protecting forests, controlling pollution, stopping illegal wildlife trade and using natural resources responsibly can help protect Nepal's endangered plants and animals. Conservation is not only the responsibility of governments and organizations — every person can contribute to protecting the natural world.
